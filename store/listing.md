## Description
Send anything to the device next to you.

Beam Connect moves photos, videos, documents, links and text between your phone and your PC in a tap — like Quick Share, but for every device with a browser. Open beam-connect.com on your phone, and your PC appears. Pick what to send, tap the device, accept on the other side. Done.

• Direct and encrypted: files travel straight between the two devices over your own Wi‑Fi (WebRTC with DTLS encryption). Nothing is uploaded to a server.
• Nearby devices find each other automatically. Devices on different networks pair once with a six-letter code or a QR scan.
• You always confirm first: every incoming request shows what is coming and a matching code on both screens. Trust a device once and it skips the prompt next time.
• Received files land in Downloads › BeamUp, as they arrive — no size limit on direct transfers.
• Text, links and one-time codes arrive with a Copy button.
• Keeps receiving from the system tray.
• Optional account: sign in on your phone and PC to see your devices anywhere and keep a transfer history. Beam Connect Pro adds a cloud relay for networks that block direct connections.

Works with Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS — the other device only needs a browser.
